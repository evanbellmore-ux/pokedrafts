# Training tab

`/training` (nav item **Training**, after Calculator): battle a practice opponent on the pinned Pokémon Showdown simulator
(c23d2e94, `[Gen 9 Champions] VGC 2026 Reg M-C`) in your browser. The build spec is
`scripts/.cache/training/design/SPEC.md` with `SPEC-ADDENDUM.md` (the addendum wins where they differ).

| Turn engine | The AI values each pairing of its candidate turn with yours on the calculator's 2v2 turn engine wherever the bridge (`sim/bridge.ts`) can describe the turn to it (an engine cell: `calculateDoublesOutcomes`, E2, on the first belief world's battle), and on simulator rollouts otherwise, a turn E2 does not estimate included. A share needs 3 actions, turns or battles as the panel shows them (the weighted count rounded, so 2.5 or more; 3 battles weigh 2.71), else "Fewer than 3 …". `KO attempts when a KO is on` is not stated: the habits do not record whether a KO was available. |

## Situations

A practice battle dealt partway through, to train recovering from a bad spot: one press and you are on turn 1 of a battle
already in progress, with ready-made teams. The page asks the worker to deal (`model/situation.ts` `SituationRequest`); the
worker deals both teams and the position (`worker/situation-deal.ts`) into a `TrainingSetup` with `situation`, and the battle
starts there (`sim/situation-start.ts`). Everything a battle has (the AI, the log, the playback, saved battles, Resume,
replays, Rematch) works from that setup.

| Topic | Fact |
|---|---|
| Setup | Above the setup, `Battle start`: `Full battle` (your teams, team preview) or `Situations` (a pressed-button pair, as the calculator's 1v1 \| 2v2). Situations shows the `Situations` card, then `Battle settings` (AI difficulty, Show the AI's read, the habits and Your trends, Information, Format and rules: the full battle's fields), whose settings a situation battle takes. The card: `Situation difficulty` (`Hard` \| `Very hard`, for every situation but a custom one), one fact line (ready-made teams from Smogon 2026-08 VGC Reg M-B usage, no team preview), `Random situation`, the named situations each with its fact for the chosen difficulty, `Fully random`, and `Custom situation`. Each starts the battle at once; while the battle rules load or a situation deals, every one stays focusable (`aria-disabled`, a press does nothing) and one status line says `Loading battle rules…` or `Dealing Down one · Hard…`; a failed deal is an alert (`Situation not dealt`). |
| Ready-made teams | Each side's six are drawn from the usage rows by their weighted set count (to the power 0.8, so the long tail is a little less rare): one per base species (Species Clause), at most two Mega forms, each with Training's usage set (`usage/suggested-sets.ts`: moves of every category, item with Item Clause in team order, ability, spread, the Protect rule). A Mega form's row gives its moves and spread, and it battles as its base species holding its stone, as a drafted Mega does. Showdown's validator checks each team; a refused Pokémon is drawn again without it (8 draws at most, else `No legal ready-made team after 8 draws.`). Members are keyed `you-<species>` and `ai-<species>`; the teams are `Your ready-made team` and `The AI's ready-made team`. |
| Named situations | The brought four are a random four of the six in a random order: the Pokémon left lead (one alone with one left) and wait in the back; the rest have fainted. HP in steps of 5 within each range. Hard / Very hard: `Down one` you 3 at 70–100% vs 4 at 70–100% / you 3 at 40–80% vs 4 at 80–100%; `Down two` 2 at 80–100% vs 4 at 50–90% / 2 at 60–100% vs 4 at 80–100%; `Last one standing` 1 at 90–100% vs 2 at 30–60% / 1 at 70–100% vs 2 at 50–90%; `Half health` your 4 at 50% vs 4 at 75% / vs 4 at 100%; `Set-up sweeper` 4 vs 4, the AI's strongest lead (base Attack or Sp. Atk) +2 in its attacking stat (physical or special by its damaging moves) / and +1 Speed; `Tailwind against you` 4 vs 4 with the AI's Tailwind (4 turns) / you 3; `Crippled leads` one of your leads / both burned (a physical attacker) or paralyzed, never a Pokémon its type, ability or item would make immune, cure or profit (Fire, Electric, Poison and Steel types; Guts, Limber, Water Veil, Natural Cure…; Lum Berry, the curing Berries, Flame and Toxic Orb); `Endgame` 2 at 20–50% vs 2 at 30–60% / vs 3 at 40–80%. `Random situation` draws one of the eight or a fully random one (one in nine). |
| Fully random | Pokémon left 1–4 for you (weights 1 : 2.5 : 3.5 : 3), the AI at least as many; HP 10–100% for you and 30–100% for the AI (Very hard: 10–80% and 50–100%); each Pokémon left a 15% chance of a burn, paralysis or poison it can have, never your last one (one left); each lead a 20% chance of a stage −2 to +2 in one stat (your last one only +1 or +2); each side a 10% chance of Tailwind. The worker rates each position with the AI's engine as Plays safe would decide its first turn (the solved turn's value, `DecisionStats.value`: the AI's side minus yours, about 1 per Pokémon of worth), on a battle of its own that posts nothing, and keeps the first one rated +1.6 (Hard) or +2.5 (Very hard) to +6 that the forced-loss guard passes (below); one rated above +6 is lopsided and never kept. When none of the 12 positions is kept, the ones rated below the margin go to the guard, nearest the margin first. For scale: an even 4-vs-4 at full HP rates about +0.9 for the AI, and the named situations' medians are +1.1 to +2.2 at Hard and +2.1 to +3.1 at Very hard (8 deals each, ready-made teams). |
| Custom situation | Per side (`You`, `AI`): `Pokémon left` (1–4), `HP` (100, 75, 50, 25 or 10% for each one left) and each lead's stat stages (Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, Speed, −6 to +6; the right lead's only with two or more left). The teams and which Pokémon play are dealt. Default: you 3, the AI 4, full HP (`down 3-4`). |
| Never hopeless | Every dealt position (named, fully random or custom) passes the forced-loss guard before its battle starts (`worker/worker-handler.ts` `forcedLoss`): it is played out from its start up to 4 times, each on a battle of its own that posts nothing, the engine deciding both sides as Plays safe (yours with the AI's whole team sheet open, so it stands for the best you could do; the AI's with `AI knows`), until your side has played 2 turns. A turn in which each of your Pokémon that acts only uses a protecting move (Protect, Detect, King's Shield…) is a stall, not a turn played: Protect puts a loss off, it does not make it winnable. When the AI won before then in all 4 playouts, whatever your side chose, or the AI's first decision rated its side more than +6 ahead (out of reach, as for Fully random), the position is dealt again: 12 positions at most per deal, new teams every 4, and a Random situation draws again each time. The first playout you are still in passes the position, so a fair one costs one playout; one that cannot run never refuses a position. The seeds come from the setup, so a position gets the same verdict each time where no deadline cuts the engine short (tests, Node). In a Node survey (6 positions per kind and level, no deadline) it refused 4 of 6 Last one standing positions at each level, 1 or 2 of 6 Endgame ones, 1 of 6 Down two · Hard and none of the others; a whole deal took about 1 to 2.5 s, Fully random up to 4 s and Last one standing up to 7 s. When none of the 12 passes: `Situation not dealt`, `Every position dealt was hopeless for your side. Deal again.` (custom: `Every position dealt with these choices was hopeless for your side. Give your side more Pokémon, HP or stat stages.`), as for your one Pokémon at 10% against four whose leads are +6 in Attack, Sp. Atk and Speed. The reported deal it refuses: your last Pokémon paralyzed, both of the AI's faster, no priority move, either one one-shotting it, with Protect or without. |
| In the simulator | The pinned simulator has no position loader. The host installs two one-shot hooks on its own `Battle` (only `sim/battle-host.ts` calls `installSituationStart`: boundary test). Both team preview choices are the dealt orders (the worker holds yours as a choice you sent, G1, and the AI's job sends its own; both are choice lines, so replays and Resume repeat them). Before the `start` action: the log's first line (`Situation: Down one (Hard). You have 3 Pokémon left, the opponent 4.`), each fainted Pokémon (HP 0, `faint` line, the side's fainted count, the last one as the side's faint of the turn before), and each other one's HP (`ceil(maxhp × share / 100)`, so the game shows at least the share) and status, so the leads' switch lines carry them as any switch-in does; a side with one Pokémon left keeps a fainted one in its second slot, as the start leaves a side with none. Then the start runs as usual (entry abilities, Supreme Overlord counting the fainted, a lead's weather); a Sitrus Berry at half HP or less is eaten as the battle starts, by the game's own rule. Before the first `endTurn`: Pokémon left, the leads' stat stages (`-setboost`, over what the start left, such as Intimidate), Tailwind (its full 4 turns, `-sidestart`). Then turn 1. |
| What each side sees | Both channels read the same public lines: each side sees the other's leads (HP, status, stages) and fainted Pokémon, and its own team as always; the Pokémon in the back are unknown to the other side until they come in, with their HP and status then. Your team's six and the AI's are public as at team preview (the trackers take a fainted-at-start Pokémon's species from its team, `createTracker` `speciesOf`, since its battle name is its base species'). The AI's belief counts your leads and fainted Pokémon as brought and draws the rest from your other members (even prior: your four were dealt at random). L1-situation: two battles alike but for your Pokémon in the back (its HP and status) give the AI the same inputs and decisions at every decision before it comes in. |
| AI and habits | The AI starts its battle as at team preview without choosing an order (`DecisionProvider.beginDealt`: the habits' new battle, the belief over your sheet); it plays every turn as in any battle. A situation teaches it your in-battle habits (actions, targets, moves, Mega Evolution) but not your leads or the Pokémon you bring (dealt, not chosen). |
| Battle | No team preview: until turn 1's request the screen says `Starting the situation…`. The header's fact line names the situation (`Down one · Hard · You: 3 left · AI: 4 left`). Turns count from the situation: its first turn is turn 1. A deal still dealing when another battle starts, a battle resumes or you go back to setup is dropped (its answer is ignored; a battle or a Resume also stops its ratings and playouts in the worker); a newer deal replaces an older one and stops it. |
| Saved battles | A situation battle is saved, resumed, replayed, exported, imported and rematched as any battle: its record's `setup.situation` holds what was asked for and the dealt position, and every re-run applies it again (conformance 9d). Saved battles, the Resume prompt, Last battle and the replay name it by its situation (`Down one · Hard`; the summary's `situation`). An import's situation is checked as every field (its schema, then `situationProblem` against its teams: four Pokémon a side, each its team's, none twice, fainted ones last and at least one left, stat stages only on a lead). |
| Stored | `pokedrafts:training:situations:v1:<user>`: `Battle start` (Full battle or Situations), `Situation difficulty` and the custom situation, read back as untrusted (each part falls back to its default by itself). The first account keeps what you chose before it was known unless it has its own. Situations themselves are not stored apart from the battles played from them. |

## Saved battles and replays

In this browser for now: IndexedDB, one database per account (`pokedrafts:training:battles:v1:<user>`, `anon` signed out), behind
the `BattleStore` interface (`saved/battle-store.ts`: list, get, save, remove, removeAll, export, import). An account store on the
planned `training_battles` table (below) will implement the same interface.

| Topic | Fact |
|---|---|
| Saved | Each battle from its first turn: an autosave as each turn resolves (the unfinished record) and the finished record when it ends, under the same id. Imported files. Up to 100 per account; past that the oldest are removed first (finished ones before an unfinished one). |
| Saved battles list | Setup screen, below Battle settings and the Start bar, newest first: date, your team vs theirs, result (Won, Lost, Tie, Forfeited, Unfinished), turns, the difficulty (`Plays safe`, `Reads you`), `Test settings on` when the battle had a test extra on (a summary saved before this fact existed gets it from its record, below), `Imported`; Rematch, Replay, Export and Delete (Resume and Delete for an unfinished one), each named by the teams and date (`Rematch Home vs Rival, 13 Oct 2026, 13:36`); Import; Delete all. The newest five rows show; the rest, with the same buttons, are behind `{n} more saved battles` (closed). Deletes ask first; after one the keyboard focus moves to the `Saved battles` heading, and closing a replay gives it back to that battle's Replay button (opening `{n} more saved battles` first when the row is there). Import stays focusable while a file is checked (`Checking…`). The newest unfinished battle also shows above the teams as `Unfinished battle` with Resume and Delete. With none unfinished, that place shows the newest finished battle you played as `Last battle` (an imported one stays in the list only): the teams, its facts (`Won · 7 turns · Reads you`, as a row's after the date) and Rematch and Replay, named by the teams and turns as the Resume prompt's buttons are (`Rematch Home vs Rival, 7 turns`); closing a replay opened there gives the focus back to that Replay button (to the row's when the card no longer shows that battle). |
| Rematch | A finished saved battle played again as a new battle (its own new record), from a row, `Last battle` or the replay screen's header (`Rematch`, `Export`, `Close replay`): the record's setup as it was played, both teams' sets, the difficulty, `Show the AI's read` and the information settings with their test extras (the record's facts state `Test settings on`; the replay's too); team preview offers the record's order as `Same as last battle` (an imported battle has none). The setup draft and the remembered setup stay as they were (the order you then submit becomes the last team order, as in any battle). The record is read again first (an unfinished or unreadable one: `The saved battle could not be read.`, in Saved battles' status region, and from the replay screen's header in the replay's own, the replay staying open), and the worker validates its teams again (an imported one included): refused, Setup states it under `Battle stopped`; the battle engine not loaded: `The battle engine did not load: …`. Before the worker has loaded, the battle waits for it. |
| Resume | The worker opens the sealed checkpoint, starts a battle on its seed with the AI's seed base and the habits that battle started from, and replays every choice in order: yours, and each AI job's decides (up to the turn's question, `DecisionProvider.replayTurn`, so the AI's belief and its habits are the ones it had) and choices. The log must hash as the checkpoint's, or match it in everything but its wording (a battle saved with older wording; the resumed log then takes the current wording), else `The saved battle did not re-run the same way.` Then the board and your request come back and the battle goes on; earlier turns keep their reads as shown, and a turn saved without `occupants` gets them from the re-run. A checkpoint from another browser or after the site data was cleared: `The saved battle cannot be opened in this browser.` When the stored habits changed after the autosave (another battle, `Clear habits`), the resumed battle does not write them. |
| Replay | Its own screen in `/training`. The worker validates both teams with Showdown's validator, re-runs the battle from the seed and the choice lines (drained after each choice, as the live battle was) and returns the board as each turn began and after the end, built from your channel per that battle's `You see`. When the re-run's log hash equals the saved log's, or the two differ only in wording (the saved battle is then rewritten with the re-run's wording), the board plays it with the battle's turn playback (popup, highlights, HP bars, results): Play and Pause, Previous turn (this turn's start, then the turn before), Next turn (both stay focusable at the ends, `aria-disabled`), the Turn slider (each turn, then End), 1× and 2× (2× halves every beat), the turn's lines and the AI's read (as the battle's `Last turn`: before a turn's steps, the turn that resolved before it; while they play, that turn), and the whole log. Otherwise the fact (`The re-run differs from the saved log from turn 4. The saved log is shown.`) and the saved log alone. |
| What a replay shows | What the battle showed you: your channel's log and steps, each board per `You see` (the AI's HP as the percentage it showed unless that battle used the exact HP test setting), both sides' actions and the AI's reads as they were redacted then. The log names Pokémon, never positions (`Incineroar (yours) used Fake Out → Incineroar (opponent's).`), by their battle names, so a Transform does not rename one there; a battle saved with older wording replays with the current wording when its re-run matches it line for line. Each replay board equals the board the battle posted at that point (each turn's start, the end): conformance gate 9a and L2 check it, L2 also scans every replay board against the log up to its turn, the AI's HP on every board and step (a percentage unless `You see` had exact HP) and its bench (unrevealed members look alike; brought only under the brought test setting). |
| What a record holds | Both teams' sets as played, the information settings, the difficulty, the seed and both sides' choice lines (to re-run it), the result, the turns and the log as shown. The seed and the choices are stored in the clear only once the battle ended. Until then they are in the sealed checkpoint (AES-GCM 256 with a non-extractable key kept in the worker's own IndexedDB `pokedrafts:training:keys`): the page stores it and hands it back for Resume, never reads it. Checkpoints are sealed one after another, so an older one never replaces a newer one. |
| Export | A finished battle as `training-battle-<date>-<time>.json` (`{ kind: "pokedrafts-training-battle", version: 1, battle }`, without the habits), downloaded on your click. |
| Import | An export file, untrusted: one over 2 MB is refused before it is read; then JSON, nesting (deeper than 24 levels is refused before anything walks it), kind, version (exactly 1; a newer format is refused; older ones go through `migrateSavedBattle`), format, rules (`c23d2e94`) and every field's type (dates within what a Date holds); then the worker's validator checks both teams and the battle is re-run: its log must hash as the file's or differ only in wording (the saved record then takes the re-run's lines, never the file's text), and its result and turn count must be the file's. The errors are the facts (`The file is not JSON.`, `Your team: …`, `The file's log does not match a re-run of its battle from turn 3.`, `The file's result does not match a re-run of its battle.`). An accepted file is saved under a new id (`Imported`) and opens in the replay screen. An import's check and a replay can re-run at the same time. Names and labels are text (React escapes them). |
| Storage | Every read and write is guarded. No IndexedDB, or a refused one: `Saved battles are unavailable in this browser.` and the page works without them. A full one: `Storage is full. Turn 4 was not saved for Resume.` or `Storage is full. This battle was not saved.` |

### Record format v1 (`model/saved-battle.ts` `SavedBattleV1`)

| Field | Type | Finished | Unfinished |
|---|---|---|---|
| `version` | `1` | 1 | 1 |
| `id` | string (UUID) | the battle's id | the same id |
| `status` | `"finished"` \| `"unfinished"` | finished | unfinished |
| `source` | `"played"` \| `"imported"` | either | played |
| `format`, `rules` | string | `gen9championsvgc2026regmc`, `c23d2e94` | same |
| `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | ms since 1970 | battle start, last save | same |
| `setup` | `TrainingSetup` | both teams (6 each, sets as played), `info` (`aiKnows`, `youSee`), `difficulty`, `showRead`, and `situation` (optional) for a battle dealt partway through: what was asked for and the dealt position (see [Situations](#situations)) | same |
| `turn` | integer | the last turn | the turn it resumes at |
| `result` | `{ result: "win" \| "loss" \| "tie"; forfeited }` \| null | set | null |
| `seed` | `"sodium,<32 hex>"` \| null | set | null |
| `inputLog` | string[] \| null | the simulator's choice lines (`>p1 team 1, 2, 3, 4`, `>p2 move 1 2, switch 3`) | null |
| `log` | `LogTurn[]` | every turn: lines, steps, actions, the AI's read as shown, and each slot's Pokémon when the turn was chosen (`occupants`, optional) with its name on that turn's board (`names`, optional) | the turns before `turn` |
| `resume` | `{ sealed }` \| null | null | the worker's sealed checkpoint (base64) |
| `habitsBefore`, `habitsAfter` | `HabitsData`, `HabitsRecord` \| null | null | the habits before the battle (its end comparison) and the ones its last turn left (Resume checks them) |
| `order` | number[] \| null | your team order (a saved battle's Rematch offers it) | your team order (Rematch after Resume) |

The list reads a summary per record (id, status, source, dates, both team labels, turn, result, difficulty, and `testSettings`,
whether a test extra was on), stored beside it. A summary saved before `testSettings` existed still reads; the list fills it in
once from its record (`battle-store.ts`: the summary is replaced only while it still lacks the field, in one transaction, so a
newer save is never overwritten; an unreadable record leaves it as it is).
A record of a 10–20 turn battle is 30–60 KB (conformance gate 9 reports the largest).

### Planned database table

```sql
create table public.training_battles (
  id uuid primary key,
  user_id uuid not null references auth.users (id) on delete cascade,
  version smallint not null default 1,
  status text not null check (status in ('finished', 'unfinished')),
  source text not null check (source in ('played', 'imported')),
  format text not null,
  rules text not null,
  created_at timestamptz not null,
  updated_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  own_label text not null,
  opponent_label text not null,
  difficulty text not null check (difficulty in ('safe', 'reads')),
  turn integer not null check (turn >= 0),
  result text check (result in ('win', 'loss', 'tie')),
  forfeited boolean not null default false,
  seed text,
  input_log text[],
  setup jsonb not null,
  log jsonb not null,
  resume jsonb,
  habits_before jsonb,
  habits_after jsonb,
  team_order smallint[],
  check ((status = 'finished') = (seed is not null and input_log is not null and result is not null)),
  check (octet_length(log::text) + octet_length(setup::text) <= 2097152)
);
create index training_battles_user_updated on public.training_battles (user_id, updated_at desc);
alter table public.training_battles enable row level security;
create policy training_battles_select on public.training_battles for select to authenticated using (auth.uid() = user_id);
create policy training_battles_insert on public.training_battles for insert to authenticated with check (auth.uid() = user_id);
create policy training_battles_update on public.training_battles for update to authenticated using (auth.uid() = user_id) with check (auth.uid() = user_id);
create policy training_battles_delete on public.training_battles for delete to authenticated using (auth.uid() = user_id);
```

The list selects the summary columns (`id`, `status`, `source`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `own_label`, `opponent_label`,
`difficulty`, `turn`, `result`, `forfeited`) as the IndexedDB `summaries` store does; `get` reads the row; the 100-row cap moves
to the store's save (or a trigger).

Left for the database version: the account `BattleStore` over this table; moving this browser's saved battles into the
account once; Resume on another device (the checkpoint is sealed with this browser's key, so an unfinished battle resumes only
here: the server would hold the key and unseal only for the worker); sharing a replay by link (the export file shares one now).
